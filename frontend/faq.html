Frequently Asked Questions

Last updated: 2026-10-17

Is NPlayStudio really free?

Yes. Every tool is free with no account, no watermark and no payment wall. The server is paid for by advertising.

What is the maximum file size?

512 MB per upload by default. Merge accepts up to 10 files. The limit is configurable by whoever runs the instance.

How long are my files kept?

One hour after the task is created. A background job then deletes both the upload and the output. Download what you need before then.

Do you look at my files?

No. Files are handed straight to FFmpeg or Pillow for the conversion you asked for. Nobody opens or watches them, and they are never shared or sold. Only anonymous metadata (tool used, status, byte sizes, and a random browser id so you can see your own recent activity) is stored, to power the dashboard.

Which video formats are supported?

Input: MP4, MKV, WebM, AVI, MOV, M4V, WMV, FLV, MPEG. Output: MP4, MKV, WebM, AVI, MOV. Codecs available are H.264, H.265 and VP9, with AAC, Opus, MP3 or Vorbis audio depending on the container.

Which audio formats are supported?

MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC, OPUS, AAC, OGG and WMA. You can also extract audio from a video file.

Which image formats are supported?

JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP and TIFF, for compression, conversion and cropping.

How do I get the smallest video?

Pick the Small preset, or set a CRF around 28–32, and drop the resolution one step. VP9 in WebM usually beats H.264 on size, but takes longer to encode.

Why is my WebM output bigger than expected?

VP9 encoding is CPU-heavy and uses a longer analysis window. Give it a slightly higher CRF, or use H.264 in MP4 if size matters more than codec modernity.

Can I merge clips with different resolutions?

Yes, but they must be re-encoded. Tick "Force re-encode" and optionally choose a normalise resolution. Stream-copy merging only works when every input shares codecs and parameters.

Does cropping preserve transparency?

Yes, for PNG, WebP, GIF and TIFF. JPG has no alpha channel, so transparency is flattened.

Why did my job fail?

Usually a corrupt or unsupported source file, or a mismatch between codec and container. The error message on the tool page names the specific problem.

Can I use this commercially?

Yes, for files you have the rights to. See the Terms & Conditions.

Do you have an API?

The web app uses a documented REST API — interactive docs are served at /docs. Automated bulk use requires prior agreement; see fair use in the terms.
