Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-17

NPlayStudio ("we", "us") provides a free online media toolkit at nplaystudio.example.com. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and how you can control it.

What we process

  • Uploaded files. Files you submit are stored on our server only long enough to run the requested conversion, compression or crop. They are deleted automatically one hour after the task is created. We do not inspect, share, sell or use their contents for anything else.
  • Task metadata. We keep a record of the tool used, the job status, and the input and output byte sizes. This powers the dashboard statistics and lets us detect abuse. It does not contain file contents.
  • Server logs. Our web server and reverse proxy record IP address, user agent and requested URL for security and troubleshooting.

Cookies and advertising

We use cookies for essential functionality (for example remembering your light or dark theme preference and your cookie-consent choice). These are not used for tracking.

We also display advertising through Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info or youronlinechoices.eu (EU).
  • For more detail see How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

See our Cookie Policy for the specific cookies involved.

Legal bases and your rights

Where the GDPR applies, we process task metadata and logs on the basis of legitimate interest in operating and securing the service, and advertising cookies on the basis of your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by clearing your browser storage for this site.

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data we hold about you, and you may object to processing. Email support@nplaystudio.example.com and we will respond within 30 days. See also our GDPR page.

Children

This service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected in the "last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to support@nplaystudio.example.com or use our contact page.
