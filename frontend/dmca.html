DMCA Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-17

NPlayStudio respects intellectual property rights and responds to valid notices under the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA).

We do not host public content

Files you upload are private, used only to run your requested job, and deleted automatically one hour later. There is no gallery, no sharing link, and no way for a third party to browse another user's uploads. Because of this, a takedown notice about a specific stored file is usually moot — the file no longer exists.

If you believe the service is being used to reproduce your work at scale, or that our own pages reproduce your material, tell us and we will act.

Filing a notice

Send a written notice to dmca@nplaystudio.example.com including all of the following:

  1. Identification of the copyrighted work you say has been infringed.
  2. The exact URL or description of where the alleged infringement occurs on this site.
  3. Your full name, postal address, telephone number and email address.
  4. A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  5. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorised to act on the owner's behalf.
  6. Your physical or electronic signature.

Counter-notice

If your content was removed and you believe that was a mistake, send a counter-notice with the same level of detail to the address above. We will forward it to the original complainant and may restore the material if they do not file a court action.

Repeat infringers

We may block access for users who repeatedly infringe copyright.

Misrepresentation

Be aware that knowingly making a material misrepresentation in a DMCA notice may make you liable for damages, including costs and attorney's fees.
