GDPR Compliance

Last updated: 2026-10-17

This page summarises how we handle personal data for visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland.

Who is the controller

The operator of nplaystudio.example.com is the data controller. Contact: privacy@nplaystudio.example.com.

International transfers

Advertising is delivered by Google, which may process data outside the EEA under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. See Google's own documentation for details.

Your rights

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification — ask us to correct inaccurate data.
  • Erasure — ask us to delete your data. Note that uploaded files are already gone after one hour.
  • Restriction and objection — ask us to limit or stop certain processing.
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw consent — for advertising cookies, clear this site's storage and choose "Essential only" next time.
  • Complain — lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority.

To exercise any right, email privacy@nplaystudio.example.com. We respond within 30 days.

Data minimisation

We deliberately do not require an account, do not ask for your name or address, and do not keep file contents beyond the processing window. There is very little personal data to protect because we collect very little.

Security

Traffic is served over HTTPS. Uploads are stored in per-task directories and removed by a scheduled cleanup job. Access to the server is restricted to the operator.
